3. Community activation

4. Registration, verification and key recovery

Use an email address you control. Review these terms and the Licensing Privacy Notice before submitting the form. You can correct the address before submission. Registration and verification are offered in English; the required local-language information must also be made available where applicable.

After you request a license, we send a verification link that expires after 30 minutes. Opening the link alone does not issue a key: you must explicitly confirm verification. A successful confirmation displays your activation key once. The current service sends the verification link by email, not the resulting activation key.

Keep your key confidential. If it is lost, verify the same mailbox again to obtain a replacement; the previous activation key is replaced. We record the accepted terms version with the request and license record. You may request a copy of the applicable terms and your service information by contacting us.

6. Connectivity, availability and expiry

The timestamps in the issued signed record determine the actual period.

An outage does not immediately invalidate an existing signed record. Once its permitted period ends, the client restricts licensed operations, including new campaigns and delivery. We do not promise uninterrupted availability or a response-time guarantee for this free service.

Revocation prevents new signed updates. A previously issued offline record may remain usable until its signed grace deadline; suspension and installation resets are not an immediate remote shutdown mechanism.
