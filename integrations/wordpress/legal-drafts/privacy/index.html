2. Information processed by this feature

  • Your email address, accepted terms version, request purpose (registration or recovery) and the time-limited verification request.
  • License identifier, status, limits, expiry, creation time and an identifier for the activated installation.
  • Application version and the most recent activation or refresh time.
  • Cryptographic hashes of verification, activation and refresh credentials. Verification emails necessarily contain the usable, short-lived link. After a confirmed recovery, the replacement activation key is also sent to the verified mailbox.
  • Security events such as issuance, activation, refresh and administrator actions, with timestamps and relevant identifiers.
  • The connecting IP address for request handling and abuse prevention. The plugin’s rate-limit table stores time-bucketed keyed hashes rather than raw IP addresses. This does not make all processing anonymous.

The licensing API does not request your campaign messages, recipient lists, collected credentials or campaign results. This statement is limited to the licensing endpoints. Information you place in a support email is also received by the support mailbox.
