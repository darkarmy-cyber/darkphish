6. Connectivity, availability and expiry

Registration requires a current browser with JavaScript enabled, access to your mailbox and completion of the anti-bot check. Activation requires a compatible DarkPhish release and HTTPS access to the licensing API. Keep the installation clock accurate and install relevant security updates.

Initial activation requires an internet connection. Activated clients periodically request a signed validity update. The current implementation supports a validity period of up to 30 days followed by up to 30 days of offline grace, both bounded by the license expiry date. The timestamps in the issued signed record determine the actual period.

An outage does not immediately invalidate an existing signed record. Once its permitted period ends, the client restricts licensed operations, including new campaigns and delivery. We do not promise uninterrupted availability or a response-time guarantee for this free service.

License expiry and an offline grace period are different: grace never extends the license's overall expiry. Renewal is not automatic. Continued access after the license expires requires an administrator to renew it and the client to obtain a fresh signed update.
